Overview

The workshop will span four days, beginning on the morning of September 14th and concluding with lunch on September 17th. On-site registration takes place in the conference room and can be completed at any time during lunches or coffee breaks by contacting the organizer in person. Please note that all lunches, coffee breaks, and the social reception are provided by the organization.

Below you will first find the schedule, followed by the titles and abstracts of the talks. The schedule is fully interactive: click on a speaker's name in the timetable to view their talk details. Conversely, clicking on the talk title or the speaker's name within the abstract cards will redirect you back to the corresponding time slot in the schedule.
