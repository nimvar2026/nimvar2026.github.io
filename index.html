About the Workshop

The workshop aims to present the outcomes of the "Nonlocality in image denoising and other variational problems" research project while providing a valuable gathering opportunity for mathematicians active in the calculus of variations. It specifically targets researchers focusing on nonlocal problems and image processing. The scientific program will feature invited lectures, and potentially a limited number of contributed talks and a poster session dedicated to early-stage researchers.

The workshop is supported by Fondazione Cariplo, grant n° 2023-0873.
